## Research Experience

### Multi-center Hip X-ray Intelligent Detection Project
**Student Lead** · March 2024–Present · Xi'an, China

Collaborative clinical research with Xi'an Honghui Hospital and Shandong Qilu Hospital, focusing on deep-learning-based detection of hip osteophytes from X-ray images.

- Led algorithm design and dataset construction.
- Achieved **97.7% mAP@0.50** on the internal test set and **92.4%** on an external hospital validation set.
- One SCI manuscript is under review, and one national invention patent application has been filed.

### State Key Laboratory of Fire Science, University of Science and Technology of China
**Research Member** · October 2023–January 2025 · Hefei, China

- Helped construct a continuous multimodal indoor-fire detection dataset combining video and physical sensing data.
- Participated in controlled fire experiments, multimodal data acquisition, and algorithm development.
- The dataset has been released publicly.
