## Research Experience

### Multi-center Hip X-ray Intelligent Detection Project
**Student Lead** · Mar 2024–Present · Xi'an, China

- Lead algorithm design and dataset construction for collaborative clinical research with Xi'an Honghui Hospital and Shandong Qilu Hospital.
- Develop deep-learning methods for detecting hip osteophytes in X-ray images.
- Achieved **97.7% mAP@0.50** on the internal test set and **92.4%** on an external-hospital validation set.
- One SCI manuscript is under review, and one national invention-patent application has been filed.

### State Key Laboratory of Fire Science, University of Science and Technology of China
**Research Member** · Oct 2023–Jan 2025 · Hefei, China

- Conducted multimodal data collection and algorithm research in controlled indoor-fire experiments.
- Helped construct the first continuous multimodal indoor-fire detection dataset combining video and physical-sensing data.
- Participated in controlled-fire experiments, multimodal data acquisition, and algorithm development; the dataset is publicly available.
